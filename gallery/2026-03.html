The Caves of Miarah

Mar 2026

Watch Gameplay on YouTube

A precision platformer created for my Portfolio class. I wanted to create something in the vein of Celeste, with the primary goal of pushing the player to be able to do increasingly difficult tasks they otherwise would not have thought they could do. You use a pickaxe to scale and break walls, and jump off moving blocks to gain extra momentum. Made in GameMaker.
