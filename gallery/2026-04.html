The Mystic Necklace of Bronze

Apr 2026

An asset sheet created for my Advanced Concept Art class. We were to create an artifact, with three variations - the bronze necklace is able to do everything its variants can, but the Zap, Chill, and Flame variants are only able to create spells of their element - but much, much greater than the original ever could.
