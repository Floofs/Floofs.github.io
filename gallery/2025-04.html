Portfolio Resume

Art Styles Challenge

Apr 2025

A set of headshots of the site's mascot, created for my Sequential Illustration class. We had to draw a character in several styles throughout the semester, including our own style. After my own style, the styles chosen in order were Adventure Time, Fox and the Hound, My Little Pony, Steven Universe, Powerpuff Girls, Studio Ghibli, Gravity Falls, Pokemon, Naruto, Tim Burton, Butch Hartman, Abstract, Cubist, The Simpsons, Dr. Seuss, Sonic Adventure, Puyo Puyo Tetris, Fire Emblem, and Metal Gear Solid.
